Workflow

How a visit flows through HealthDesk

Patient Intake

Reception searches by phone, registers if new, selects doctor & slot, and confirms the booking.

Queue Assignment

Same-day bookings auto-create a queue token. Live position is visible to both staff and patients.

Consultation

Doctor opens the patient's diagnosis form, records findings, and writes a prescription — closing the appointment.

Billing & Payment

Bill is generated automatically. Patient can pay online via Razorpay or at the counter. PDF issued on completion.
